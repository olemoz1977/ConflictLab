## 2. Reuse principle

The V2 unit remains a forced choice between two images, but the images do not have to be pixel-matched twins.

Preferred future pattern:

```text
same meaningful decision context
+ two legitimate alternatives
+ different benefits / motivational affordances
+ similar overall visual quality
+ repeated across independent scene families
```

Matched pairs remain useful in LAB validation and as perceptual controls.
