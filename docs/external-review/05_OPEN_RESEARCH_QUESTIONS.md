# 05 — Open Research Questions

These questions are not answered by the current implementation. They define what future testing and research would need to address.

1. Does the person understand the choice task without additional explanation?
2. Do the pre-written cues help the person name their reaction, or do they impose wording that was not actually theirs?
3. Does the literal reflection of chosen words (Stage I) help the person notice anything?
4. Does the person want to return for multiple sessions?
5. Does the history view help the person notice change over time?
6. Does the radar convey meaning without the author explaining it?
7. Do repetitions across multiple sessions carry more meaning than chance repetition would?
8. How strongly do low-level visual properties of the stimuli (contrast, brightness, framing) drive the choice, independent of any intended content dimension?
9. Does the person distinguish between "I do not see it yet" and "I prefer not to name it," or do these feel equivalent to them in practice?
10. Does the system indirectly interpret the person anywhere in its current wording or flow, even unintentionally?
11. What control stimuli would be needed to separate content-driven choice from low-level visual artifact?
12. Does variation in context (time of day, mood prompt, framing of the task) change the choice pattern?
13. How many sessions are needed for a meaningful intra-individual comparison?
14. Are the person's own written words more valuable as data than the internal vector layer derived from cue selection?
