# 00 — Tester Protocol

**This is a small usability and reflection-value test, not a psychometric validation study.**

This document instructs the tester conducting the test. It exists to ensure all five participants are tested consistently and that the tester does not contaminate results with explanations.

## Participant plan

- 5 participants
- Minimum 3 sessions each
- Sessions should be completed at different times when practical. This is preferred, not a condition for inclusion in this small usability test.

## Estimated time

- Each session: approximately 3–5 minutes
- Post-test interview: approximately 10–15 minutes

These are estimates, not requirements. If a real session takes substantially longer, treat that itself as a UX signal worth noting.

## Tester role

The tester **may**:
- Help open the link
- Help with export/import if technically necessary
- Note where the participant pauses or asks a question
- Ask only the pre-specified questions in this protocol

The tester **may not**:
- Explain what the images mean
- Explain the cues
- Explain the radar
- Suggest which choice to make
- Explain what the participant "should have noticed"
- Interpret the participant's answers
- Say that the system detected a trait
- Encourage a specific answer

## Intervention rule

If a participant pauses or gets stuck:

1. Wait at least 10 seconds
2. Ask: **"What is unclear right now?"**
3. Do not answer on behalf of the system
4. If the participant genuinely cannot continue without help, provide only a technical instruction (e.g., "tap that button")
5. Record the intervention on the Session Observation Form (Section G)

## Session order

### Session 1
- Participant reads the participant instructions
- Participant completes the session independently
- After the session, only briefly note points of confusion
- Do not explain the result

### Session 2
- Performed at a different time
- No additional methodological explanation
- Observe whether the participant remembers the flow

### Session 3
- Performed at a different time
- After the session, the participant views their history
- The radar is shown only if the system presents it naturally (i.e., only if eligibility criteria are already met)
- If the radar does not appear, this is not a participant failure — skip the radar-related interview questions entirely and mark "not shown" on the Results Summary
- The tester does not explain the radar
- The post-test interview follows this session

## Areas being tested

- Task comprehension
- Naturalness of the choice
- Usefulness of cues
- Use of free text
- Distinction between "not seen yet" and "prefer not to state"
- Value of history
- Value of the literal chosen-word reflection
- Comprehensibility of the radar
- Willingness to return
- Any misleading or overly strong language
- Understanding of privacy and data controls

## Data handling

- Do not use real names in the results file
- Use participant IDs: P01, P02, P03, P04, P05
- Do not record sensitive personal information
- Do not copy a participant's free text into a public report without their consent
- Keep test files local
- If a shared device is used, clear Pair P0 data between participants, or export and clear

## Stop conditions

Stop the test if:
- The participant wants to withdraw
- The participant shows clear emotional discomfort
- The participant believes this is a diagnosis or evaluation and, after clarification, still does not want to continue
- A technical problem prevents continuation
