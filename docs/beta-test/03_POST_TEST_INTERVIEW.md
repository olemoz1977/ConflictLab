# Post-Test Interview

Conducted after Session 3. Questions must be open, neutral, and free of leading suggestions.

## Opening

"I am interested in how you understood and experienced the tool. There are no correct answers."

## Core questions

1. What did you think the tool was asking you to do?
2. What did you think the image choices represented, if anything?
3. How did you decide between the two images?
4. Did the suggested wordings help you describe your reaction, or did they influence it?
5. Was there a moment when the tool helped you notice something you had not named before?
6. Was there a moment when the tool seemed to say more than it could actually know?
7. How did you understand the difference between not seeing a connection yet and preferring not to name it?
8. Did seeing your chosen words together add anything?
9. Did the session history help you notice anything across sessions?
10. What did you think the radar meant before anyone explained it?
11. Did the radar add value, confusion, or neither?
12. Did any text feel like a judgment, diagnosis, or personality label?
13. Would you use this again without being asked?
14. What would make you return?
15. What would make you stop using it?
16. What was the most useful part?
17. What was the least useful or most confusing part?
18. What should be removed?
19. What is missing?
20. In one sentence, what do you think ConflictLab currently does?

## Follow-up rules

**Allowed neutral follow-ups:**
- "Can you tell me more?"
- "What made you think that?"
- "Where did you notice that?"
- "What did you expect to happen next?"

**Forbidden follow-ups:**
- "Did it reveal your need for safety?"
- "Did you notice a pattern?"
- "Do you think the radar was accurate?"
- "Was this your System 1 response?"
