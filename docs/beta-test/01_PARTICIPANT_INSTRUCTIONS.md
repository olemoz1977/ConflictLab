# Participant Instructions

## What this is

ConflictLab is an experimental reflection tool. It is not a personality test, diagnosis, or evaluation.

## What you'll do

You will see several pairs of images. Choose the one that draws you first. There is no correct answer.

## After choosing

You may choose a wording that fits your reaction, use your own words, or leave it unnamed.

## Sessions

Please complete three short sessions at different times. Try not to do all three in one sitting.

## Privacy

Your session history is stored in this browser. You may export it, import it in another browser, or clear it before another person uses the device.

## An important limit

The system does not know why you chose something. It only shows back the choices and words you provided.

## Your rights

You may skip, stop, or choose not to name a reflection at any time.
